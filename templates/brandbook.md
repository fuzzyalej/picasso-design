# Brandbook

## Foundation
- **Mission:** one sentence.
- **Vision:** one sentence.
- **Values:** four to five, each one sentence.
- **Personality:** about five words.
- **Positioning:** one sentence.

## Verbal Identity
- **Voice Essence:** "[Brand] sounds like [character]: [quality], [quality], [quality]."
- **Tone axes:** Formal/Casual, Calm/Energetic, Serious/Witty, Accessible/Authoritative.
- **Voice qualities:** five or six, each with a Do and a Don't.
- **Vocabulary:** words we use, words we avoid, proprietary terms.
- **Writing style:** sentence length, punctuation, capitalization, numbers, contractions.
- **Things We Never Say:** explicit list.

## Visual Identity
- **Logo:** direction, clear space, minimum size.
- **Color:** table of name, value, role.
- **Typography:** hierarchy table.
- **Imagery and iconography:** direction.
- **Design principles:** three or four.
