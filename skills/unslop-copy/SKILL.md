# Unslop copy

Structure, not vocabulary, is the deepest tell. Work three passes, cheapest first.

## Level 1: mechanics
No em-dashes or en-dashes. One quote style. Sentence-case headings. Bold only where it earns attention. Do not turn prose into `**Term**:` bullets. No emoji. No "In summary" or "Conclusion" restatement. Strip chatbot debris such as tracking query strings and placeholder `[Your Name]` tokens.

## Level 2: vocabulary and rhetoric
Cut inflated significance ("stands as a testament", "plays a vital role"), promotional tone ("boasts", "seamlessly", "nestled in the heart of"), negative parallelism ("not just X but Y"), reflexive rule-of-three, filler analysis ("highlighting the importance of"), vague attribution ("experts argue"), didactic disclaimers ("it is important to note"), dated AI vocabulary (delve, tapestry, leverage, robust), mechanical connectives (Moreover, Furthermore), and empty merisms ("from startups to enterprises"). Watch for clean slop: an aphoristic one-liner closing every paragraph, "That is not X. That is Y.", uniform maximum confidence. Budget at most one aphoristic close per piece.

## Level 3: structure and epistemics
Do not state the moral or recap. Break the claim-support-takeaway symmetry. Use real specifics (names, sums, dates) rather than invented ones. Leave some endings open. Leave slack: a sentence or two at half pressure. Epistemics are non-overridable: never invent numbers, quotes, or facts for liveliness; hedge once, not per sentence.

## Outline test
Read the first sentence of each paragraph. If they form a clean summary on their own, the structure is machine-shaped; rework it.
